feat(sync): limit retries with a per-client retry budget

Each request could retry up to max_attempts times on its own, so a
failing backend drew retries from every in-flight request at once and
the client kept adding load while the backend was down.

SyncClient now shares a token-bucket RetryBudget across all of its
requests. The budget holds retry_budget retries (default 10) and refills
at that rate per minute. When a retry is needed and the budget is empty,
send() fails at once instead of sleeping and trying again.

Both budget exhaustion and running out of attempts now raise
RetryBudgetExceeded with the request id, in place of the generic
SyncError("exhausted attempts"). It subclasses SyncError, so existing
handlers still catch it. The client no longer sleeps after the final
failed attempt.
