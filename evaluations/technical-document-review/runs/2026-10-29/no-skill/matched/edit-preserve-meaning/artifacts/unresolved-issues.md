# Unresolved issues

The supplied materials do not resolve the following:

- **Reranker's contribution:** The pilot changed the reader, context cap, and reranker together, so it cannot attribute the accuracy increase to any individual component.
- **Uncertainty and production performance:** The pilot did not estimate statistical uncertainty or establish whether the observed increase would hold in production. It does not provide sufficient evidence for immediate replacement of the baseline.
- **Parent expansion results and schedule:** No study has run, and no evaluation date is supplied. The proposed five-percentage-point decision threshold is not an observed gain; the original claim that evaluation will occur next month is unsupported.
- **Latency:** The pilot did not measure latency, and the timing example supplies no numerical result. The example times a post-warmup invocation, leaving cold-start latency unmeasured.
